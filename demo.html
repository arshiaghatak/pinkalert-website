These results were produced by running the published models from the GitHub repository (Random Forest, MLP and SVM, weighted 60/25/15) on 21 distinct images drawn from the public Breast Ultrasound Images (BUSI) dataset by Al-Dhabyani et al. Images that duplicate another scan, or whose expert outline misses a visible lesion, were swapped for other scans; every tumor outline comes from the dataset's expert annotations. The model outputs are shown exactly as produced: 19 of the 21 match the expert label, including the misses. The same dataset was used to train the models, so these samples demonstrate behavior rather than serve as an independent test.

Medical disclaimer. PinkAlert is a research prototype and educational tool. It is not a medical device, has not been cleared by the FDA, and does not diagnose cancer. Always consult a qualified clinician about any breast changes or concerns.
