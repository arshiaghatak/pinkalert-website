Questions

Good to know.

When will PinkAlert be available?

PinkAlert is a research prototype being developed one tested generation at a time, and there is no release date yet. Everyone on the launch list will be told first.

Does signing up cost anything or commit me to buy?

No. Signing up is free and only means you'll get an email when PinkAlert launches.

Is PinkAlert a medical device?

Not yet. It has not been cleared by the FDA and does not diagnose cancer. It is being designed as an early-warning aid that tells you when to see a doctor.

Can I try it today?

Yes, in a way: the Live Demo runs the PinkAlert analyzer on real ultrasound scans, and the analyzer code is open source.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask to be removed at any time.
